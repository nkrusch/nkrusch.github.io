Research

I am generally interested in static reasoning about programs (by some interpretation) and ways to ensure programs are correct (by some metric). My topics of interests include program analysis, implicit computational complexity, and formal methods. For a summary of my doctoral research, have a look at this extended abstract. I am a former professional software engineer, which means I like artifacts and seeing theory implemented in practice.
